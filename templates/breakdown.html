Week Fantasy Breakdown

Every stat below is a real trailing team average (last up-to-8 games) from nflverse's public closing lines, box scores and play-by-play, ranked 1-32 across the league. Tempo and pass rates are neutral-situation (quarters 1-3, within 14 points, outside the last two minutes of the half) and season-to-date once a team has played 2 games, since schemes change year to year; salaries and trends are DraftKings' and this app's own real player data. All analysis text is generated from those numbers by DFSRiches itself -- not reproduced from any outside publication.

DFS Model

Consensus of public projection sources (each source's stat line scored with DraftKings rules), then only backtested adjustments. Consensus is always shown before adjustments; sourced facts and model estimates are labeled. Nothing is filled in when a source is missing a player.

Add projected ownership

No projected-ownership source is connected, so ownership is blank unless you paste it here (one per line: Name, 23.5 or Name, TEAM, 23.5; DSTs by team code, e.g. BUF, 8). It stays in this browser and is labeled "user-provided".

Game-by-game breakdown
Data: real closing spread/total lines and team box-score trends from nflverse; real DraftKings salaries, trailing DK-style FPPG and matchup-adjusted ceilings for each team's DFS targets, when DraftKings has posted pricing for the week. League ranks are computed among whichever teams have a trailing value at this point in the season.
