DFS Model

Lineups built to fit together, not just the best nine players. Cash lineups chase floor, volume and stability; GPP lineups chase ceiling, correlation, leverage and game environment. Projections are a consensus of public sources scored with DraftKings rules; every sourced fact and model estimate is labeled, and missing data (ownership, props, snaps, routes) is called out instead of guessed.

It stays in this browser and is labeled "user-provided". Chalk, leverage, contrarian lineups and lineup ownership totals all switch to it.

Projections: Sleeper, ESPN, CBS Sports, FFToday and FantasyPros (public pages), scored with DraftKings rules. Usage: nflverse box scores. Lines: nflverse closing lines. Salaries and injury statuses: DraftKings.
