DFSRiches ("we", "us") provides NFL daily fantasy sports research tools: projections,
ownership estimates and a lineup builder, on the web and in our mobile apps.

## What we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account we collect your email address
and username, and anything else our sign-in provider requires at the time (for
example a password, your name or a mobile number). We also record that you
confirmed you are 18 or older.

**Data you save.** Lineups you save, contest entries you log, your player tags
(Prioritize / Neutral / Fade), and your preferences (what you play, your weekly
budget, your last slate and tab, builder settings). We store these so they follow
you to every device.

**Usage and diagnostics.** Which screens you open and actions you take in the app
(for example finishing the intro, building a lineup, opening a definition),
crash and error reports, and feedback you send us. These are tied to your account's
internal ID, not your email address.

We do not collect payment card details ourselves. [If/when paid plans launch: payments
are processed by [Stripe / Apple / Google], which handle your card details under their
own privacy policies.]

## How we use it

To provide the service and sync your data; to understand which features help and where
people get stuck so we can improve the app; to fix bugs; to keep the service secure
(including rate limits and preventing account sharing); and to communicate with you
about your account.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party
advertising.
