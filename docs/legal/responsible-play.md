# Play responsibly (DRAFT — needs review)

Daily fantasy sports should be fun. A few habits help keep it that way:

- **Set a budget** for the week and stick to it. Settings → Your game lets you note what
  you usually spend; Best contests then favors entry fees that fit.
- **Never chase losses.** Projections are estimates, and even the best lineup loses often.
- **Take breaks**, and keep fantasy from getting in the way of work, family or sleep.

**If fantasy sports or gambling stops being fun, help is free and confidential:**
call or text **1-800-GAMBLER** (National Council on Problem Gambling) or visit
[ncpgambling.org](https://www.ncpgambling.org/help-treatment/).

DFSRiches is for adults 18 or older (21+ where state law requires).
